LINE Bot development has no single fixed price. Conversation branches, data sources, permissions, external integrations, and acceptance criteria all change the implementation scope. A useful quote starts with a defined workflow.

Define the workflow first

Messages and conversations

List triggers, reply branches, fallback behavior, and when a person should take over. Rules, AI-generated replies, and human handoff are separate parts of the scope.

Data and integrations

Identify whether the Bot reads or writes Google Sheets, a CRM, booking, orders, or an internal API. Permissions, formats, and failure handling affect each integration.

Administration and acceptance

Decide who can change content, which actions need records, and what scenarios define an accepted delivery.

What a quote may include

  • Discovery and workflow: interviews, flow definition, edge cases, and acceptance criteria.
  • Development and integration: Bot logic, data storage, external APIs, permissions, and error handling.
  • Third-party services: LINE, model APIs, hosting, and other providers charge by their current plans or usage.
  • Post-launch service: monitoring, corrections, content changes, and new features require an agreed responsibility scope.

Bring these details to a quote discussion

  1. Where do users enter, and what are their three most common goals?
  2. What data must be read or written, and where is it stored now?
  3. When should a person take over, and which team receives the notice?
  4. Who owns content, platform accounts, hosting, and later changes?
  5. Which realistic scenarios must pass acceptance?

FAQ

How much does LINE Bot development cost?
Cost depends on conversation flows, data sources, permissions, integrations, and acceptance scope. AutoDev currently uses NT$50,000, NT$100,000, and NT$200,000+ as project budget positions, then quotes against the confirmed scope.
How long does LINE Bot development take?
The schedule is assessed after the workflow, integrations, data readiness, and acceptance method are confirmed. The quote lists delivery stages and scope.
What other LINE Bot costs should I plan for?
Possible costs include LINE platform fees, model APIs, hosting, and third-party services. These follow actual usage or provider plans, while maintenance scope and fees are stated separately in the quote.
Telegram is AutoDev’s main service. Can you still build a LINE Bot?
Yes. Telegram Bot is our main service, and we still take LINE Bot projects when LINE fits the audience or an existing official account. Channel choice is reviewed during discovery.
