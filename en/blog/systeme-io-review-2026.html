Systeme.io Review 2026

📅 2026-05-03 · ⏱️ 0 min read

What is Systeme.io?

Systeme.io is an all-in-one marketing platform: email marketing, sales funnels, online courses, affiliate program, blog, automation. The free plan covers most solopreneur needs.

Free Plan Limits

  • Up to 2,000 email contacts
  • Unlimited emails
  • 3 sales funnels
  • 1 online course
  • Affiliate program
  • Blog with unlimited posts

Paid Tiers

Startup $27/mo (5,000 contacts, 10 funnels), Webinar $47/mo (10,000), Unlimited $97/mo. Compared to ClickFunnels ($147/mo) or Kajabi ($149/mo), much cheaper.

Best For

  • Course creators starting out
  • Affiliate marketers needing landing pages + email
  • Small e-commerce + lead-magnet style businesses
  • Testing funnel ideas without committing $$$

Not For

  • High-volume e-commerce (use Shopify)
  • Complex multi-product membership sites (use Kajabi/Thinkific)
  • Large enterprise email lists (use Mailchimp/SendGrid)

Why We Recommend It

Systeme.io's free plan is genuinely usable, not crippled. We use it ourselves for lead magnets and email sequences. Sign up free (affiliate link, 60% lifetime recurring commission).
